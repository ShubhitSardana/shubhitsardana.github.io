About & Education

I recently completed my BS-MS dual degree in physics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal, focusing on gravitational-wave astrophysics, continuous gravitational waves, and pulsar astronomy.

Later in 2026, I will join Swinburne University of Technology and the ARC Centre of Excellence for Gravitational Wave Discovery (OzGrav) in Melbourne for my PhD. My doctoral research will center on continuous gravitational waves from rapidly rotating neutron stars and the intersection of gravitational waves, pulsar timing arrays (PTAs), and observational cosmology.

As an EPTA collaborator and InPTA associate member, I develop Bayesian pipelines to isolate correlated noise processes, constrain supermassive black hole binaries, and construct targeted standard-siren cosmological distance indicators.
