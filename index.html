Research Interests

01

Continuous Gravitational Waves

Searching for resolvable CW signals from compact and supermassive binaries with PTA observations.

02

Pulsar Timing Arrays

High-precision pulsar timing, correlated noise processes, and inference of nanohertz gravitational-wave signals.

03

Observational Cosmology

Using gravitational-wave sources as standard sirens to constrain H0, ΩM, and related cosmological parameters.

04

Bayesian Inference

Hierarchical models, prior marginalisation, and computational methods for robust inference from PTA datasets.

About & Education

I recently graduated with BS-MS dual degree in Physics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal, an Institute of National Importance. My master's thesis was focused on the effects of solar-wind variability on pulsar observations.

As an associate member of InPTA and collaborator with the researchers at EPTA, I contributed to the development Bayesian inference scripts for targeted standard-siren cosmology to constrain H0 and/or ΩM, and continuous wave searches. I also worked on the ensemble noise properties of pulsars in EPTA DR2+ dataset and Gaussian-process modelling of the solar wind for the InPTA DR2 Noise Analysis paper.
